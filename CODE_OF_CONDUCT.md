# Code of conduct

Urbiva exists to make cities work for everyone. We expect the same care in how we work together.

## Our standards

- Be respectful, patient and constructive, in every language.
- Assume good intent and ask before concluding.
- Write and design with accessibility in mind: alt text, plain language, sufficient contrast.
- Credit the work of others accurately.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, discrimination, insults or personal attacks, including about disability, age, gender, ethnicity, religion, nationality or any other characteristic.
- Publishing another person's private information, or photos of identifiable people, without consent.
- Sharing credentials, personal data or confidential information in issues, pull requests or discussions.
- Misrepresenting sample data, plans or research as real-world results.

## Scope

This code applies in every repository, issue, pull request and discussion of the urbiva organization, and when representing Urbiva elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the organization owners through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove content, and may warn, restrict or block participants who break this code.
